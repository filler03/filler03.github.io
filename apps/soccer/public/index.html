Pick a code, share it with a friend. When you both enter the same code, the match kicks off — one shared ball, a goal at each end, and both of you can kick at any time.

One taps Play to host and set the rules; the other enters the same code to join. Swipe up through the ball toward your goal to kick — longer, faster swipes hit harder. There are no turns: whoever gets to the ball can kick it.

iPhone browsers can't go fullscreen. For an app-like experience: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen, then launch from the home screen.
